the audit of the existing code against its written rules, the bugs found with their evidence, the fixes made in

parameter defaulting to 0.

## What was checked and found correct

- **Timezone.** `data/history/.../M1.parquet` `open_time` is true UTC: the M1 bars 2025-11-05 13:58-14:01 UTC equal
  the bid ticks of the same UTC minutes (open/high/low/close identical). Tick files are UTC days.
- **MT5 ATR** in `tr1/bars.py` equals `precursors/foundation.py` (TR[0] undefined, first value at bar 14).
- **Fractals / pivots**: radius-1, equal lows/highs allowed, usable only after the right-hand bar closes.
- **Anchors**: Asia 07:00 and London 13:00 Thai, New York 08:30 DST-aware; H = ATR(14) of the last H1 bar closed by
  the anchor.
- **TR1 entry**: first raw quote at/after the decision bar's close within 5 minutes, ask for BUY / bid for SELL;
  stop = pivot -/+ max(fill spread, 0.10 x M1 ATR at the decision bar); risk band 0.05-0.50 H.
- **TR1 SPLIT exit** (`tr1/exits.py`): stop first on each quote; 1/3 at exactly +1R; M5 trail, graduation to M15 at
  +3R; stops only tighten; first quote at/after 8 h; gap stops fill at the quote. Re-derived independently for 19
  trades (below): identical.
- **Exam guard** in `tr1/boundaries.py` / `tr1/data.py` (P2 and the exam locked; tick days checked before reading).

## Ambiguities kept as in the original (please confirm)

- **A1 Signal price for SELL.** V1 builds SELL signal bars as bid + the M1 `spread` (an ask proxy); BUY uses bid bars.
  E0 is the executable fill (ask for BUY, bid for SELL), compared with bid closes for BUY. Both sides therefore need
  about one spread extra to "reclaim". The rule does not say which price the M5 closes use.
- **A2 "Next M1 open".** Implemented as the first quote at/after the confirmation close, within 2 minutes;
  otherwise `NO_ENTRY_QUOTE`.
- **A3 "Pivot formed after candidate entry".** The M5 bar opening at the confirmation close (the entry minute's bar)
  counts as formed after the entry.
- **A4 Windows count existing M5 bars** (across the daily break / weekend), as the original.
- **A5 Eligibility includes a trailed stop before +1R even with R > 0** (rule wording "stopped before +1R").
- **A6 Slippage** applies to the entry only (TR1 FOUNDATION), default 0.
