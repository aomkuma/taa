# Review: Trend Runner / RETEST-CONTINUATION V1, and how to develop it further with the PLAN-1 learning method

Written 2026-10-22 by a research reviewer. This is a read-and-recommend review: no strategy code was changed, no rule was
re-run, nothing was committed. Every number below was taken from the files named next to it and, where a trade file
exists, recounted with pandas from that file (scripts in the reviewer's scratchpad, not in the repo). Times are UTC
unless marked Thai (UTC+7). Results are event-level R after the recorded spread; **slippage is excluded** in every
number here (owner's cost rule: spread only). Past results describe the sample only; none of this is a claim of future
profit.

## 1. Factual status

### 1.1 What the setup is

The family has two layers.

1. **TR1 (Trend Runner), the source** (`research/2026-10-20-trend-runner/PLAN.md`, `HANDOFF.md`): XAUUSD; after an
   opening impulse of 0.25 completed-H1 ATR at the Asia (07:00 Thai), London (13:00 Thai) or New York 08:30 anchor, the
   first causal M1 pullback/continuation entry; SPLIT/RUNNER exit (1/3 at +1R, 2/3 on an M5 structure trail that moves
   to M15 after +3R; 8-hour maximum). TR3 is a monthly RUNNER/SKIP selector on top of it.
2. **RETEST-CONTINUATION V1** (this folder, `PLAN.md`): when the TR1 RUNNER is stopped *before* first touching +1R,
   wait up to 8 h for an M5 close back beyond the original entry E0 (reclaim), then within 6 M5 bars a retest of E0 that
   closes on the reclaimed side, then within 2 M5 bars a close beyond the retest bar's far extreme; enter at the next M1
   open. Stop = retest extreme minus max(entry spread, 0.10 x M5 ATR(14)); no target; trail on confirmed radius-1 M5
   pivots; 8-hour maximum. In PLAN-1 language this is **a codified re-entry after a SWEPT stop**: the original stop was
   taken, price came back through the entry, retested it and continued.

### 1.4 Open problems

1. **Sample size and pace.** Neither period reaches 100 attempts. At ~3.8 a month the prospective plan's interim
   (50 attempts) is about 13 months away and its formal review (100 attempts / 60 days) about 26 months away.
2. **Outlier dependence.** Both periods are positive only through 1-3 trades of +10 R or more; the median trade is
   about -0.25..-0.38 R. The edge, if any, is "rarely catch a long run", which needs many attempts to measure.
3. **Fidelity.** Both V1 periods are M1 OHLC replays. The TR walk-forward measured an M1 uplift of about +0.06..+0.07 R
   per trade for TR1 SPLIT; if V1's bias is similar (not yet measured), the holdout mean of +0.023 is below zero on
   ticks. Ticks exist for every V1 attempt (section 2), so this is checkable.
4. **The +30.96 R figure** belongs to a source-stop-time screen; the correct entry-time gate gave +0.132 R and does not
   pass. Do not cite +30.96 R as evidence.
5. **Independence.** The 2021-23 gold was used by PLAN-1 old-year studies as well; and the TR1 base outcomes on
   2021-23 were computed during the holdout run (only stopped-before-+1R rows were written).
6. **Not a calm-gold filler.** The whole holdout is calm gold (closed-D1 ATR(20) median 24.5 USD, every attempt < 50;
   section 3). V1 lost there without its single best trade, so it does not cover the regime where PLAN-1 stops.

- **Gold has no untouched historical window left.** The only gap is a sliver around 2023-10 that overlaps the TR

## 3. Descriptive regime and context reading (reviewer, 2026-10-22)

Closed-D1 ATR(20) (simple mean of the true range, the same definition as `agents/journal/regime_switch.py`) and the
D1 20-day direction (sign of close vs the close 20 D1 bars earlier) attached at each attempt's entry. **This reading
used the holdout, so the holdout is now even more clearly consumed: nothing below may be confirmed on it.**

| Cut | Holdout 2021-23 (n, R, ex-best-1) | Dev 2023-26 (n, R, ex-best-1, ex-best-3) |
|---|---|---|
| side **with** the D1 20-day direction | 46, +7.43, **-5.27** | 49, +39.71, +26.36, +6.66 |
| side **against** it | 48, -5.23, -7.47 | 59, -11.47, -14.08, -18.45 |
| D1 ATR(20) < 50 | 94, +2.19, -10.51 | 72, +10.54, +0.92, -8.11 |
| D1 ATR(20) >= 60 ("large gold") | 0 | 31, +18.96, +5.60, -8.81 |

Readings:

- **Against the D1 direction lost in both periods, and after removing best trades.** This matches the owner's
  framework (D1 = the side with the edge) and was not derived from TR outcomes, so it is a fair pre-registered
  hypothesis - but on the holdout the "with" side is positive only through one trade.
- The BUY/SELL asymmetry is mostly this: holdout BUY-with +17.84 (18), SELL-with -10.41 (28) - so 2021-23 SELL "with
  trend" also lost; the side effect is not purely D1 direction.
- **PLAN-1's regime switch does not transfer cleanly.** Large gold (>= 60) is positive in dev only through two trades
  (+13.36 at ATR 78.5, +10.08 at 85.3); 2026 attempts (median ATR 173) were -0.23 R on 14.
- **V1 and PLAN-1 overlap little in time:** of V1's 21 dev attempts inside PLAN-1's learning span, 3 overlapped an open
  PLAN-1 position, though 20 were on a PLAN-1 trading day; entry hours spread from 02 to 21 Thai.
- **Stops are small:** holdout median initial risk 2.20 USD (p90 4.04), dev 3.82 (p90 15.10). Against a gold spread of
  about 0.2-0.3 USD (PLAN-1 CHECKLIST row 58) the round-trip spread is roughly a tenth of a median holdout R; this is
  the "calm gold, few-USD trades" cost trap seen in PLAN-1 row 58. At a 100-USD account and 7.5 %, the 0.01 minimum lot
  is not binding (2.2 USD stop = 2.2 % at 0.01 lot; about 0.03 lot fits), unlike PLAN-1 where wide stops made 0.01 lot
  over-risk.

## 4. Recommendations, ranked by expected value

1. **Tick-fidelity audit of the 202 existing attempts (94 holdout + 108 dev) before any new design.** Same frozen V1,
   replayed on the D: and `data/ticks` day files for the attempt windows only, exam days skipped; report M1 vs tick R
   per attempt, sign agreement and the mean shift. It is an audit (no rule change, no new evidence claim), it is cheap
   relative to new research, and it decides whether anything is left: if the tick mean is <= 0 in both periods, V1 as
   frozen should be ticked FAIL in the checklist with that lesson. Run it only when the machine is free (not beside the
   DEMO stack). Planted-bug and independent re-derivation checks as for every PLAN-1 component.
2. **A PLAN-1-style weekly journal on the development cohort (2023-11 .. 2026-04-16), as the learning set.** For every
   V1 attempt: the source stop's position against the pools everyone sees (Asia range, previous-day H/L, last M15
   swing, round 10/5 USD), the source stop's class (SWEPT - came back and ran; FAKE - ran on against; SLOW - drifted),
   the reclaim speed, D1 ATR(20) and D1 direction, H1 ATR, MFE/MAE and when, why it won or lost, and "what would have
   made this loser pay" (transform before discard: same points with a wider stop / nearer first target / opposite
   side). Each week: what the winners share, the repeating weakness, the **rules that cost this week**, and at most one
   change, checked on all prior weeks and applied forward only; delete a rule when its net over all prior weeks turns
   negative. **Adaptation for V1's pace:** with 1.5 attempts per active week, a weekly change has almost no evidence
   (PLAN-1 row 63: 8-week lever re-selection lost to frozen rules). So journal weekly, but let a change enter the
   frozen candidate only after >= 30 attempts of support and not on one outlier (show every cut without the best 1 and
   3 trades).
3. **Pre-register the D1-side hypothesis (V1-D1) and test it once on the untouched FX set, then on forward gold.**
   Owner-rooted, consistent sign in both used periods, cheap. Pass criteria in section 5. Do not test it on the 2021-23
   holdout (consumed) and do not use the exam.
4. **Reach >= 100 attempts by cross-instrument transfer, not by reusing gold.** EURUSD / GBPUSD / USDJPY M1
   2023-09-01 .. 2026-04-17 (exam months excluded). Freeze the FX adaptation first (point size, spread from the M1
   spread column, the same Thai anchor windows, relative ATR units unchanged), plus a code-correctness round (unit
   tests, planted bugs, independent re-derivation of sampled trades). Read once. If the number of attempts there is
   far from the gold pace, report it - that itself is a finding about the mechanism.
5. **Regime: record, do not switch yet.** Carry D1 ATR(20) on every journal row. Declare the >= 60 split as a secondary
   pre-registered cut only (descriptive in section 3 shows it depends on two trades). If the tick audit and FX test
   fail in calm conditions too, note that V1 does not fill PLAN-1's calm-gold gap.
6. **Complement to PLAN-1: decide only after V1 passes.** The time overlap is small (3 of 21), so V1 could add trades on
   PLAN-1 days at other hours; but its PLAN-1-span result was -3.61 R on 21. A joint one-account replay (the owner's
   "tune alone, judge together") belongs after a pass, not before.
7. **Keep the prospective observer running untouched** (first 50 attempts not read for tuning), but do not wait on it:
   it is the slowest evidence (about two years to 100).

What must NOT be done:

- Re-tune V1, the ATR gate, the side/session filter or the D1-side cut on the 2021-09-01 .. 2023-10-02 holdout (used,
  and used again descriptively in this review).
- Open the exam [2026-04-18, 2026-10-18) without the owner's explicit release.
- Cite +30.96 R (source-stop-time screen) or +28.25 R (in-sample dev) as evidence.
- Re-select levers week by week on a short window (PLAN-1 row 63).
- Merge historical diagnostics into the prospective candidate journal, or write into the Champion shadow database.
- Present any result without "slippage excluded" and without the ex-best-1 / ex-best-3 lines.

## 5. Proposed next-step plan (for the owner's approval)

**Step A - tick-fidelity audit (no new evidence, no rule change).** Replay frozen V1 on ticks for the 94 holdout and
108 dev attempt windows; exam days skipped. Output: per-attempt M1 vs tick R, mean shift, sign agreement. Gate: if the
tick mean is <= 0 in both periods, V1 is recorded FAIL with the lesson; Step C then tests only a journal-derived V2.

**Step B - weekly journal on the dev cohort** (learning set, already open), PLAN-1 format, as in recommendation 2.
Output: the journal, the rules-that-cost lines, and at most two candidate changes frozen with hashes.

**Step C - one read on the untouched FX set**, with this pre-registration written and hashed before the run:

> PRE-REGISTRATION - RETEST-CONTINUATION V1-D1, cross-instrument read, frozen on <date/time UTC> before any FX
> Trend Runner run.
>
> Hypotheses: H1 (primary) - frozen V1, taking only attempts whose side agrees with the sign of (last closed D1 close
> minus the close 20 closed D1 bars earlier), has a positive mean R net of the recorded spread. H2 (secondary) - frozen
> V1 without the D1 cut. H3 (secondary, descriptive) - the D1 ATR(20) split, in the instrument's own percent-of-price
> terms, decided by the bands written here before the run: <bands>. Plus at most two journal-derived changes from
> Step B, each written out in full here.
>
> Data: EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDJPY M1 from `data/history/FBS-Demo`, source events 2023-09-01 .. 2026-04-17 UTC, attempts
> whose 8-hour horizon crosses 2026-04-18 excluded. No gold. No exam. Read once; no parameter is changed after the run.
> FX adaptation frozen here: point size per `spec.json`, spread from the M1 spread column, same Thai anchors, all
> distances in ATR units as in V1.
>
> Costs: recorded spread only; slippage excluded and stated. M1 OHLC, stop first on ambiguous bars.
>
> Pass (all required, for H1): >= 100 completed attempts on >= 60 distinct days pooled; mean >= +0.15 R; positive in
> both chronological halves; day-block bootstrap 95 % interval of the mean above zero; positive without the best 3
> trades; at least two of the three symbols positive. Reported in any case: per symbol, per year, per week (Thai
> weeks), side, window, MFE/MAE, funnel counts, ex-best-1/3, worst run of losses.
>
> On a pass: forward gold observation of V1-D1 from the next Monday, 50 attempts untouched, then the one-account joint
> replay with PLAN-1. On a fail: CHECKLIST row with the lesson and the next transform (wider stop / nearer first
> target) designed on the dev journal, never on the FX data just read.
